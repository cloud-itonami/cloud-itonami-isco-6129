# Business Model: Animal Producer Farm Scheduling and Logistics Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-6129`
- ISCO-08: `6129`
- Occupation: Animal Producers Not Elsewhere Classified
- Social impact: animal-welfare-integrity, worker-safety,
  farm-logistics-transparency

## Customer

- animal-producer farms / livestock operations
- independent farm workers/handlers

## Offer

- feeding-schedule/animal-condition-check-in record logging
- crew/task scheduling
- feed/supplies procurement coordination
- always-escalating welfare-concern flagging channel

## Revenue

- monthly farm retainer
- per-record logging fee

## Trust Controls

- worker/farm record must be independently verified and registered before
  any action
- no proposal, anywhere in the closed op allowlist, can finalize an
  animal-treatment/welfare/breeding decision, or override a farm safety
  officer's judgment — these are structurally absent, not merely gated
- any animal-welfare or injury-risk concern surfaces ONLY via
  `:flag-welfare-concern`, which always escalates to a human
- feed/supplies orders above the registered per-farm cost ceiling always
  require human sign-off
- work, scheduling and supply records are auditable, not editable
